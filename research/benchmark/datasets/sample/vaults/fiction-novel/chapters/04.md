The cat appeared in the alley behind A Brasileira on a wet evening in November. She was, technically, not Maria's. The owner of the cafe disagreed and said the cat had chosen her. Maria conceded the point.

She had been calling the cat Whiskers in her head for the first two days, but the cat clearly did not approve. Maria renamed her Olivia, after a friend who had also disliked Whiskers, and the cat seemed to consider this acceptable.

It was the longest paragraph she had written in months — though, of course, no one would ever read it. The manuscript was gone. This one she kept.
